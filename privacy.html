Zero Sixty privacy policy

Effective 31 October 2026

In short: Zero Sixty doesn't collect, store or share any personal data of its own. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and your progress stays on your device. If you use Google Play Games on Android, Google handles that data (see below). How to delete your data.

This policy covers Zero Sixty, the endless highway pursuit game made by Piyush Mutha, in both its Android app and its web version at piyushmutha.github.io/zerosixty.

What the game keeps, and where

The game saves your progress so it's there next time: your best score and distance, total distance driven, the paints you've unlocked and picked, and your settings. These are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage (or your browser's local storage on the web). They're never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes them.

What the game doesn't do

  • It doesn't ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.
  • It has no ads, no advertising ID, no analytics and no crash reporting of its own.
  • It doesn't sell or share data with anyone.

Google Play Games (Android)

On Android the game can use Google Play Games, which may sign you in automatically if you have a Play Games profile. When you're signed in, the game reads your Play Games name to show who's signed in, and sends your scores to the game's leaderboard. That data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; the game doesn't store or send it anywhere else. You can delete this game's Play Games data, or your whole Play Games profile, in the Play Games app or your Google Account (steps below).

Deleting your data

Zero Sixty has no servers and no accounts of its own, so there's nothing to ask us for: you can delete all of it yourself.

Progress on your device: best score and distance, total distance, paints and settings.

  • Android: uninstall Zero Sixty, or go to Settings > Apps > Zero Sixty > Storage (or Storage & cache) > Clear storage.
  • Web: clear the site data for piyushmutha.github.io in your browser.

It's deleted straight away, and no copy is kept anywhere, because it never leaves your device.

Google Play Games data: this game's link to your Play Games profile and your scores on its leaderboard.

  1. Open the Play Games app.
  2. At the top of the screen, tap More, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Play Games account & data.
  4. Under "Delete individual game data", find Zero Sixty and tap Delete.

The same screen can delete your whole Play Games profile. Google deletes this data from its servers under the Google Privacy Policy; Zero Sixty's developer keeps no copy of it. Google's own steps, including how to do it from the Play Store or the web, are in Delete your Play Games data or Play Games profile.

Permissions

  • Internet, for Google Play Games. The game itself plays offline.
  • Vibration, for haptic feedback in crashes and stunts. You can turn it off in Settings.

The web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The game doesn't use cookies.

Children

Zero Sixty isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
